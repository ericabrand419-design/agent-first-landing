The philosophy

Stop grading people on decisions the organization never made.

The worker switches between five tabs, searches for a rule, asks a supervisor, and keeps a customer waiting. Then we call it an employee performance problem.

I start somewhere else. We observe what the employee actually does, clarify the decisions and rule ownership, and design better guidance around the moment of work.

AI and software are tools, not the starting point. Sometimes the answer is simply a clearer procedure, a better knowledge structure, or a decision the organization finally needs to make.

The responsibility problem

Training cannot fix a decision the organization never made.

Too often, the person closest to the customer is furthest from the people designing the work. They compensate for missing policies, disconnected information, and unclear authority—live on a call.

My work is to stop putting that burden on the frontline.

“Why are people still being asked to remember things the system should know?”

I started asking that question after seeing capable frontline employees struggle through complicated rules, scattered information, and decisions their organizations never clarified. Then those same people got graded on the results.

I didn't start Agent First because the world needed another app. I started because frontline workers deserve systems that support their expertise instead of testing their memory.

Design the System Around the Human. That's the consulting philosophy. Keep the Human. Upgrade the Agent. That's the flagship product promise.
