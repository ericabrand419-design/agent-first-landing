Agent First started by questioning the system around the worker.

EB

Erica Brand

Founder, Agent First

Agent First Consulting is the advisory and research side of the same operating philosophy behind the Agent First product.

Frontline organizations routinely ask people to be fast, accurate, compliant, empathetic, and consistent while expecting them to navigate fragmented knowledge, client-specific rules, scripts, exceptions, and multiple systems in real time.

That creates a familiar management response: more training, more documentation, more reminders, more coaching.

But there is another question worth asking: What if the worker is not the primary problem?

Agent First began with the observation that many performance failures are actually failures of the decision environment. The person at the frontline is being asked to assemble the correct answer live — often while a customer is waiting — even when the organization already knows the context, rules, wording, and next step that should shape that answer.

Agent First Consulting turns that observation into a practical discipline: study the work, identify what the employee is being forced to figure out, redesign the environment, and preserve human control where judgment still matters.

Consulting + product

The consulting work is not a disguised software pitch.

A diagnosis may point to better documentation, fewer systems, training, workflow changes, automation, Agent First software, or a combination. When the product does fit, the consulting work creates a much stronger implementation foundation because the real decision environment has already been mapped.
