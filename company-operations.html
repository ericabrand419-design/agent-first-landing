The operating infrastructure behind Agent First.

One company operating system for formation, IP, contracts, privacy, security, billing, Recallya, records, vendors, and the paid-pilot launch gate.

Current operating architecture

Start with one founder-owned U.S. operating entity under a cleared legal name. Agent First, Agent First Consulting, and Recallya remain distinct products or business lines under that entity unless financing, liability, ownership, tax, or acquisition considerations create a real reason to separate them.

PRODUCT

Agent First

SERVICES

Paid-pilot launch gate

Do not treat “the demo works” as commercial readiness. A paid pilot starts only after the legal, financial, data, security, billing, access, backup, vendor, and incident-response basics are operating.
