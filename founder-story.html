Founder story

Agent First did not begin as “I want to build an AI startup.” It began with a practical frustration: why does a frontline worker have to become the integration layer between the customer and the organization? The worker is expected to know which client is active, find the right rule, interpret the exception, remember what can and cannot be promised, translate that into customer-facing language, and still sound confident while a live interaction continues.

The more Erica looked at the problem, the less it felt like a training problem or even a knowledge-base problem. It was an interface problem between organizational rules and human action.

That led to the design principle behind Agent First: software should absorb more of the organizational complexity so the worker can spend more attention on people, judgment, and action. AI can help with interpretation, retrieval, structure, and wording, but the organization remains the authority on its rules and the human remains the operator.

Founder thesis: before organizations automate humans away, make the human dramatically easier to equip.
