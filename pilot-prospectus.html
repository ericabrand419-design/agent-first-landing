Path

From evaluation to production pilot.

1

Discovery + source intake.
Select the workflow, sources, users, baseline and rule owner.

2

Controlled configuration.
Configure and test with approved, fictional or de-identified material where appropriate.

3

Security Readiness Gate.
Appsec review, threat model/data flow, isolation/RBAC testing, remediation, retest, data boundary and sign-off.

4

Live pilot.
Bounded 90-day production-data pilot only after the gate passes.

5

Weekly reviews.
Metrics, issues, rule changes, defects, security notes and scope.

6

Final readout.
Continue, expand, revise/retest or stop based on evidence.

Security + data

Right information. Right client. Right moment. Right person.

Before proprietary production procedures, client-specific operating material, sensitive production context or regulated information enters Agent First, an experienced security-minded SaaS engineer or appsec consultant reviews the actual architecture and data flow. The gate includes server-side role authorization, tenant/Client Profile isolation testing, authentication/session review, database/API/secret/upload/model-provider review, logging, backups, retention/deletion, incident response, OWASP ASVS mapping, remediation of critical findings and retest.

Important: OWASP ASVS and NIST SSDF are verification and development references, not certifications. SOC 2 and sector-specific compliance follow when buyer requirements justify them.
