Who we accept

The right early pilot is difficult enough to matter and bounded enough to learn from.

Rules-heavy

Repeated decisions where the correct answer changes by client, account, location, service, product, or exception.

Source-ready

Actual approved policies, scripts, exceptions, contacts, and a person empowered to validate what the organization means.

Measurable

Enough workflow volume, users, or controlled scenarios to establish a useful before/after comparison.

Sponsored

What gets measured

Five numbers tied to real operational cost.

Targets are agreed before launch. They are measurement targets, not promises of improvement.

Decision resolution time

How long it takes to get from a recognized rule question to a validated decision.

Correct-client rule application

Whether the rule applied belongs to the active Client Profile and scenario.

First-contact accuracy

Whether the customer receives the correct actionable answer the first time.

Supervisor pull-ins

How often agents still need a human supervisor for in-scope rule questions.

New-hire ramp time

Where applicable, how quickly newer agents reach the agreed proficiency threshold.

Evidence standard

The final report shows the baseline, final result, measurement method, limitations, and negative findings too.

Security + data

Minimize the data. Keep policy authority with the organization.

Agent First should use only the data needed for the scoped workflow and measurement plan. Recordings or transcripts are used only when the customer is authorized to use them for the pilot and the agreed handling is appropriate. The organization owns and approves its rules. Agent First should not claim security or compliance certifications it has not earned; hosting, retention, subprocessors, encryption, and regulated-data requirements must be verified against the production architecture before they are represented contractually.
