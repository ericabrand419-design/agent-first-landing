Customer FAQ · v1.0

What buyers usually need to know.

Product, pilot, implementation, data, pricing, measurement, and what happens if the evidence is not strong enough to continue.

What is Agent First?

Operational decision support for rules-heavy frontline work. It turns approved organizational rules into context-specific decisions, customer wording, next steps, and source-backed guidance.

Is Agent First replacing our agents?

No. The current product is designed to assist the worker. The organization owns its rules, and the human remains the operator.

Does Agent First invent our policies?

No. Approved organizational sources and rule-owner validation are the authority. If sources conflict or are missing, that should be surfaced rather than silently guessed.

Do we have to replace our CRM, CCaaS or scheduling system?

The early pilot is intentionally configuration-first and should prove value without forcing a platform replacement. Deeper integrations can be scoped later if they are justified.

Who is a good pilot candidate?

A team with repeated rules-heavy decisions, clear operating pain, approved source material, a rule owner, representative users, a measurable baseline, and one workflow that can be bounded.

What does the pilot cost?

The live pilot may run for up to 90 days at $69 per pilot agent per month, with a 10-seat minimum ($690 per month). The standard $2,500 Client Profile implementation fee is waived for accepted pilot partners. No Growth discount applies during the pilot.

What happens if we continue?

The validated Client Profile carries forward. A one-time $500 credit applies to the first post-pilot invoice. Standard reference pricing is Starter $69 per agent/month, qualifying Growth $49, and Enterprise custom. No automatic renewal.

What do you need from us?

A sponsor, a rule owner, a bounded workflow, approved source material, representative users/supervisors, baseline access, and timely answers to rule conflicts or gaps.

What do you measure?

Typical measures include decision resolution time, correct rule application, supervisor assists, outside search, controlled scenario performance, and user/supervisor trust. Final metrics are agreed before results are known.

How long before users see it?

Configuration and user readiness happen before the up-to-90-day production live clock. Current operating planning targets roughly Days 0–18 for discovery, intake, configuration, validation and baseline, but a production pilot also waits on the Security Readiness Gate. Controlled evaluation with fictional/de-identified material can continue while security review/remediation is open.

Can you handle multiple clients or locations?

That is a core design emphasis. Client Profiles make the active context explicit. One primary Profile is included by default in the pilot; additional Profiles are separately scoped.

What data do you need?

Only what the bounded workflow requires. Before the Security Readiness Gate, use fictional or appropriately de-identified material by default. Proprietary production procedures or client-specific operating information enter a live production pilot only after independent architecture/appsec review, critical remediation/retest, and a documented permitted-data boundary.

Are you SOC 2 certified?

No certification should be inferred. The immediate requirement is a focused pre-production Security Readiness review with an experienced security-minded SaaS engineer/application-security consultant, followed by critical remediation and retest. SOC 2 or sector-specific assurance follows only when actual buyer/regulated-use requirements justify it.

What security work happens before proprietary production data?

Agent First will produce a threat model and architecture/data-flow map; review authentication/sessions, server-side role and tenant/Profile isolation, APIs/database, secrets, uploads, model-provider data flow, logging, backups, dependencies, retention/deletion and incident response; map against OWASP ASVS 5.0; and remediate/retest critical findings before Security Readiness sign-off.

Can we use our own terminology and scripts?

Yes. The point is to operationalize the organization's approved wording, rules, contacts, exceptions, and next steps.

What if a rule changes during the pilot?

Changes should be versioned, reviewed, validated by the rule owner, and retested before publication.

What if the pilot does not work?

The final readout should say so plainly. The pilot can stop with no automatic renewal. Mixed or negative evidence is more useful than forcing a conversion.

Do you have customer case studies yet?

Not yet. Agent First has product/readiness evidence, but external customer ROI and testimonials remain pending until real pilots produce them and customers approve their use.
