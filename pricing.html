Pricing
RIGHT CLIENT. RIGHT ACTION.

Simple, seat-based pricing.

One rate per agent, per month, plus a one-time setup fee to build the Client Profile that holds each client's approved rules. That's the whole model.

Plans

Starter is where every new client relationship starts. Growth is the rate you grow into as you add seats and bring more of your client roster onto the platform. Enterprise is a custom scope for the largest, most complex operations.

The setup fee

$2,500, one time, per client account.

This is what funds your Client Profile build-out: your real policy language, entered once into a structured rule set every agent can rely on from their very first call on the account. It's a one-time cost per client, not a recurring charge, and it's waived entirely for pilot partners.

A limited pilot program

Pilot partners get full access with the setup fee waived.

A small number of pilot partners are accepted at a time, each running Agent First on their own real rules and real calls, so the results mean something. If it converts, a one-time $500 credit applies to your first invoice.
