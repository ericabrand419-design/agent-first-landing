Reduce uncertainty before adding scale.

What happens after Pilot 1

Major feature work pauses long enough to analyze what actually happened. The post-pilot sprint separates product failure from onboarding failure, source-rule quality, training, measurement noise, and customer-specific quirks.

Advance

Measured value, buyer continuation/expansion, core hypothesis intact.

Repeat or narrow

Promising signal but noisy test—or value only in a smaller workflow.

Change or stop

No sustained use, no willingness to pay, or services burden structurally overwhelms software value.

Hiring, integrations, and fundraising are triggered—not scheduled.

Hiring

Use a security specialist now as scoped expertise. Hire engineering when founder/product bottlenecks are evidenced. Hire sales only after founder-led sales is repeatable.

Integrations

Build when two or more customers need the same underlying pattern, or a strong target segment treats it as a real procurement/value blocker.

Fundraising

Optional until capital can accelerate an already-working motion: repeated customer value, commercial pull, known implementation economics, and clear milestones that money unlocks.

Current rule: finish the paid-pilot launch gate → choose one strong pilot partner → lock baseline and success criteria → stabilize only what the test needs → run the 90-day paid pilot → hold the learning sprint before the next major product commitment.
