Operational decision support for rules-heavy frontline work. Current pre-seed planning target: $650,000.

01

Problem

Knowledge can exist everywhere — and still be unusable in the moment.

Frontline workers are often asked to search, interpret, remember, and translate rules while the customer is waiting. In multi-client operations, the same question can require a different correct answer depending on the client being served.

02

Solution

Turn approved organizational knowledge into the next usable action.

Agent First makes the active client/location/program explicit, then surfaces the decision, customer wording, next step, source, and internal guidance. AI can assist; the organization remains policy authority and the human remains the operator.

03

Product

A decision cockpit, not another knowledge tab.

The private demo spans five industries and ten fictional organizations with Agent, Supervisor/Trainer, and Admin/Implementation views, Client Profiles, scripts, sources, memory, Test Mode, Guided Demo, and regression guardrails.

04

Why now

Automation is removing simple work. Remaining human work gets more contextual.

BLS reports 2.666M U.S. customer service representative jobs in 2025 and about 289,500 annual openings through 2035. The thesis is not more agents forever; it is better decision support for the complex interactions that remain.

05

Beachhead

Start where context switching is a daily operating fact.

Initial wedge: multi-client contact centers and outsourced/shared-service teams with repeated rules-heavy decisions, approved sources, a rule owner, measurable baseline, and a workflow that can be proven without major integration.

06

Market sanity check

Small share of a broad labor pool can still matter — but this is not the TAM.

0.1% of broad U.S. CSR employment ≈ 2,666 seats; 0.5% ≈ 13,330; 1.0% ≈ 26,660. Account-level serviceable-market research still needs to establish real buyer counts, budgets, sales cycles, stacks, and willingness to buy a separate decision layer.

07

Business model

Pilot at the normal software rate. Continue only if evidence earns it.

90-day live pilot at $69 per pilot agent/month; $2,500 Client Profile implementation fee waived; no Growth discount during the pilot; $500 first-invoice conversion credit. Post-pilot: Starter $69, qualifying Growth $49, Enterprise custom.

08

GTM

Founder-led proof before sales-team scale.

Targeted account → workflow discovery → private demo → paid pilot → measured readout → continuation / expansion only if the evidence supports it. Acquisition engine: thought leadership, targeted outbound, interactive website/Labs, Deal Room, and partner/advisor referrals.

09

Competition

Agent First is entering a crowded category on purpose.

Five9, Genesys, NiCE, Cresta, Observe.AI, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Glean and others offer overlapping assistance, knowledge, workflow, or AI capabilities. Agent First's wedge to prove is profile-first context, governed operational rules, built-in testing/measurement, and lightweight coexistence.

10

Evidence

Built is not the same as proved.

Built: working demo, 5 industries/10 fictional organizations, three roles, private access, Test Mode, Admin workflow, regression tests, pilot package, Deal Room, Business Plan, Financial Model, Investment Ask. Not yet proved: customer ROI, conversion, retention, repeatable implementation margin, repeatable sales cycle, external case studies, enterprise certifications.

11

Roadmap

Sequence proof before scale.

Now: readiness. Next: first paid measured pilot. Then: ~3 paid pilots + ≥1 recurring conversion and lower configuration effort. Then production/security hardening. Then ~5 pilots + multiple recurring deployments and a customer-approved reference if earned.

12

Financial plan

Base case is a planning model — not a forecast.

Base model: ~$45K Year 1 revenue, ~$155K Year 2, ~$324K Year 3, ~$27.7K Year 3 exit MRR, ~$332K annualized exit recurring, ~67% Year 3 gross margin, and ~$502K peak cumulative cash need.

13

The ask

$650K pre-seed to buy proof, not premature scale.

Use of funds: $200K product/engineering; $110K security/infrastructure; $110K pilot delivery/customer success; $100K founder-led GTM; $50K legal/finance/operations; $80K reserve. Instrument and valuation/cap remain TBD.

14

Founder

Built from the frontline workflow outward.

Erica Love is building Agent First from direct observation of the gap between what organizations know and what frontline workers can use in the moment. The durable advantage must become documented product principles, customer evidence, repeatable implementation, technical systems, and a team.

15

Close

The next milestone is not scale. It is proof.

Paid pilot → recurring conversion → repeatable implementation → production/security readiness → evidence-backed next decision.
