Investor Diligence · Financial Model v1.0

Model the assumptions. Don’t disguise them.

Agent First’s first financial model translates the restored 90-day seat-based pilot into conservative, base, and upside operating scenarios. It is a planning tool, not a forecast and not evidence of customer traction.

Canonical commercial assumptions

The pilot uses the same software price the customer recognizes after the pilot.

TermCurrent modelWhy it matters
Pilot term90-day live pilot after configuration, validation, baseline, and readiness sign-off.Long enough to measure real use without creating an open-ended trial.
Pilot software price$69 per pilot agent per month.The customer is already paying the Starter software rate rather than buying a separate consulting package.
Client Profile implementationStandard $2,500 fee waived for accepted pilot partners.Agent First absorbs the initial configuration investment while learning from the design-partner deployment.
DiscountNo Growth-tier volume discount during the pilot.Protects price integrity while the deployment is still being proved.
Conversion$500 one-time credit on the first invoice if the pilot continues.Creates a modest continuation incentive without discounting the product itself.
ScopeOne bounded workflow and one primary Client Profile by default.Prevents an early pilot from turning into economically unbounded custom implementation.
Important: Conversion, churn, implementation cost, seat counts, support cost, pilot velocity, and sales cycle are management assumptions until customer evidence exists. The model is designed so those inputs can be replaced rather than hidden.

Model structure

What the workbook actually models

AreaDriverWhat must replace the assumption
PilotsStarts per year, average seats, 90-day duration, $69 seat rate.Signed pilot cadence and actual seat counts.
ConversionPilot-to-subscription conversion and $500 credit.Completed pilot decisions.
Recurring revenueEnding accounts × average seats × effective monthly seat price.Actual tier mix, expansion, contraction, and churn.
ImplementationPer-pilot labor proxy plus additional paid Profiles after conversion.Tracked founder/engineering/implementation hours.
Cost of revenueHosting/model cost, support cost, payment/collections cost.Production bills and measured support load.
Operating expensesFounder, engineering, customer success, legal/security, GTM, admin.Actual hiring/contractor plan and milestone-gated spend.
CapitalPeak negative cumulative operating cash flow.Beginning cash, financing, working capital, taxes, timing, and actual burn.
