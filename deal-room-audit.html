Completeness rules

One source of truth per topic

Pricing, pilot terms, security posture, roadmap, evidence status, and company narrative should each have a canonical current source.

Cross-links, not duplication

Pages may summarize, but detailed answers should link back to the canonical material instead of maintaining conflicting copies.

Version discipline

When a major product/commercial/security assumption changes, update the canonical source first, then dependent summaries.

Maintenance checklist

  • Every external claim points to a current evidence/support basis.
  • No page claims SOC 2, HIPAA, production-regulated-data readiness, ROI, or cross-industry validation unless actually established.
  • All private routes use the shared authentication model.
  • All CTAs have a valid next state: request access, open Deal Room, demo, pilot, or evidence.
  • Old previews and stale pricing/pilot language are retired.
  • Media shows a current stable product state.
  • Evidence feed remains empty rather than inventing proof.
