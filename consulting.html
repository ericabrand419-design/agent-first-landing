The point of view

Most companies are adding AI to workflows that were already badly designed.

The usual starting question is, “Where can we put AI?” I think that question arrives too early.

The better sequence is: understand the job, expose the decisions and information it requires, decide what responsibility belongs to the person versus the system, and only then decide where AI actually adds value.

Agent First Consulting

Independent product and work-design practice.

“Why are people still being asked to remember things software already knows?”

That question became the beginning of Agent First. I became interested in the enormous amount of human work that is really searching, remembering, switching systems, reconstructing context, interpreting rules, and carrying procedural complexity in working memory.

My work starts there. Not with the newest model. Not with a feature list. With the person trying to do the job—and the question of what the surrounding system should be responsible for instead.

The result is part operating-model design, part product design, part knowledge architecture, and part applied AI. The goal is simple: make difficult work easier to do correctly without designing the humanity out of it.
