Public website notice

Privacy & demo data handling

Agent First offers public product information, fictional demonstrations, business calculators, and inquiry forms. This page explains the boundaries of those public experiences; it does not replace a customer-specific production data agreement.

Contact and pilot inquiries

When you submit a contact or demo-request form on this website, the information you choose to enter is submitted through the Web3Forms service to route the inquiry to Agent First. This may include your name, contact details, organization, and written message. Do not include passwords, regulated records, personal customer information, or proprietary policies in these inquiry forms.

Product demonstrations

Public demonstrations use fictional Client Profiles and illustrative operating rules. The connected/production version of Agent First has not been independently validated for customer production data. Do not enter actual client records, patient information, payment data, or confidential organizational procedures into public demonstrations.

Site usage and third parties

This website uses third-party hosting and includes Google measurement/analytics tags. External platforms linked from the site may apply their own privacy terms. We do not currently publish a comprehensive, audited data-retention schedule or completed production security certification for Agent First.

Business evaluation and production readiness

For a production deployment, data access, vendors, retention, deletion, customer rule approval, and applicable legal/security obligations must be established in a scoped agreement and reviewed before proprietary or regulated production information is introduced.

Questions or data requests

For inquiries about information submitted through the website, including access, correction, or deletion requests, email info@myagentfirst.com. Requests are reviewed based on the systems involved and applicable obligations; no instant or automatic deletion is implied.

For demo users: keep all sample content fictional or appropriately de-identified. The production-data security gate has not been completed for a public demo sandbox.
