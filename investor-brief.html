The problem

Frontline workers can have access to the right information and still be unable to use it quickly enough. In multi-client work, the same request may require a different correct action by client, location, rule version, or exception.

The product

Agent First makes the active operating context explicit and surfaces the decision, customer wording, next action, source, and internal guidance. AI assists with interpretation/retrieval/structure; the organization remains policy authority and the human remains the operator.

Beachhead + market

Initial wedge: multi-client contact centers / outsourced shared-service operations. BLS reports ~2.666M U.S. CSR jobs in 2025 and ~289,500 annual openings through 2035. This is a scale proxy, not Agent First's TAM.

Base 36-month management model: ~$45K / $155K / $324K revenue in Years 1–3; ~$27.7K Year 3 exit MRR; ~67% Year 3 gross margin; ~$502K peak cash need. Planning scenario, not forecast.
