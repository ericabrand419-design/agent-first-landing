The problem

Frontline workers can have access to the right information and still be unable to use it quickly enough. In multi-client work, the same request may require a different correct action by client, location, rule version, or exception.

The product

Agent First makes the active operating context explicit and surfaces the decision, customer wording, next action, source, and internal guidance. AI assists with interpretation, retrieval, structure and wording; the organization remains policy authority and the human remains the operator.

Security gate

Agent First is evaluation-ready with fictional or de-identified material. Before proprietary production procedures or sensitive client-specific operating material enter a live pilot, the Security Readiness Gate requires architecture/appsec review, threat model/data flow, server-side authorization and tenant/Profile isolation testing, AI-provider data-flow review, OWASP ASVS mapping, critical remediation, retest and pilot data-boundary sign-off.

Beachhead + market

Initial wedge: multi-client contact centers / outsourced shared-service operations. BLS reports ~2.666M U.S. CSR jobs in 2025 and ~289,500 annual openings through 2035. This is a scale proxy, not Agent First's TAM.
