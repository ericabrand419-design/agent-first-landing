Measurement / evidence

Pilot Measurement & Evidence Standard

The standard for separating product facts, hypotheses, raw pilot observations, reviewed evidence, permissioned public evidence, and repeatable evidence.

Evidence levels

LevelMeaningAllowed use
E0 · Product factObservable current product behavior or documented company control.Describe factually if current.
E1 · HypothesisExpected workflow/value effect not yet demonstrated with customer outcome evidence.Explain as hypothesis or pilot objective.
E2 · Raw observationPilot measurement or qualitative signal that has not completed review.Internal analysis only.
E3 · Reviewed evidenceBaseline/result/method/limitations reviewed and supportable for the stated scope.Internal decisions; customer-specific reporting.
E4 · Permissioned public evidenceE3 plus explicit customer permission for the specific public claim/logo/quote.Website, Deal Room, sales/investor materials within approved wording/scope.
E5 · Repeatable evidenceSimilar effect observed across multiple independent customers/workflows using the same core product model.Broader commercial/category claims with appropriate limits.

Baseline rules

  • Define the metric before live results are visible.
  • Document population, time period, inclusion/exclusion, source, calculation, and owner.
  • Use the same measurement logic in baseline and pilot whenever feasible.
  • Do not move the success threshold because the result is disappointing.
  • Record known workflow changes, staffing changes, seasonality, and other confounders.

Core pilot metrics

Decision resolution time

Time from the point a qualifying decision question becomes actionable to the point the agent reaches the operationally correct next action.

Search / supervisor dependence

Share of qualifying interactions requiring extra policy search, supervisor assistance, or escalation to resolve the decision.

Accuracy / QA

Rate of correct rule application, required wording/disclosure, routing, scheduling, or documentation under the defined audit method.

Training / ramp

Time or practice required before a new/less-experienced user reaches the agreed competency threshold.

Adoption

Share of eligible work where agents use the intended Agent First workflow, plus abandonment reasons.

Account-launch effort

Time/effort required to configure a new customer/client rule set without product-specific engineering forks.

Attribution and limitations

Agent First should not claim that a change was caused by the product merely because it occurred during the pilot. Document training changes, staffing mix, policy changes, seasonality, volume mix, coaching, other tooling changes, and measurement limitations. When causal confidence is weak, use narrower wording: “observed during the pilot” rather than “Agent First caused.”

Review and publication flow

Measure

Baseline + weekly/final result + qualitative observations.

Review

Method, source, sample, confounders, limitations, and the exact statement supported.

Permission

Customer approves any quote, logo, identifying detail, or public outcome statement.

Publish

Only reviewed + permissioned items enter the public Evidence Register feed.

Propagate

Approved evidence may appear on the website, Deal Room, sales/investor materials, and as support for model-assumption review.

Retire

Withdraw or narrow evidence that becomes outdated, contradicted, or no longer permissioned.

Financial model rule

Evidence does not silently rewrite the forecast. Reviewed pilot evidence may justify a founder/CPA decision to change conversion, implementation, retention, pricing, support-cost, or expansion assumptions. The model change must identify the evidence and retain the previous assumption/version.

Case-study standard

  • Customer/workflow scope
  • Baseline and method
  • Pilot term and population
  • Observed result
  • Known limitations/confounders
  • Customer-approved quote/logo language
  • No implication that one customer represents an entire industry
