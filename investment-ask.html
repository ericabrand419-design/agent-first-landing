01 · The Ask

Why $650,000

Raise against proof milestones, not because startups are supposed to raise.

Target raise
$650K
Pre-seed planning target.
Base-model need
~$502K
Peak cumulative cash need across the current 36-month Base planning model.
Timing cushion
~$148K
Protects the proof plan from slower conversion, security work, or delivery variance.
Capital thesisThe round is intended to buy external proof and repeatability: a focused independent security architecture/application-security engagement before proprietary production data; remediation and a hardened pilot environment; paid production pilots; recurring conversion; lower implementation effort; and enough customer evidence to make the next financing or operating decision from facts.

The ask is intentionally larger than the modeled Base cash need because the model is still built from unvalidated conversion, churn, implementation-cost, support-cost, and sales-velocity assumptions. Sizing the round exactly to the model would treat those assumptions as certainty.

Before a larger round, Agent First should be able to say:

  • Customers have paid to test Agent First in real workflows.
  • At least some pilots converted into recurring software usage.
  • Implementation time and deployment cost are becoming measurable and repeatable.
  • The active buyer/ICP is clearer than “rules-heavy frontline teams” in the abstract.
  • The pre-production Security Readiness Gate has been completed and the resulting architecture/threat model, remediation record, isolation tests, data boundary, and incident process are documented for target-buyer diligence.
  • Measured customer evidence exists with limitations, not just product demonstrations.
06 · Risk Discipline

What Could Make the Ask Wrong

A real use-of-funds plan should make clear which evidence would cause the company to spend less, spend differently, or stop.

Risk signalCapital response
Pilots do not close at $69/agent/monthRevisit ICP, value metric, qualification, and pilot structure before increasing GTM spend.
Implementation remains highly bespokeInvest in configuration tooling and narrow scope; do not scale concurrent delivery until unit economics improve.
Suite vendors are “good enough”Narrow to workflows where profile/context governance or deployment model creates a meaningful difference.
Security review finds material architectural issuesDelay proprietary production-data pilots, remediate the findings, continue controlled evaluation with fictional/de-identified material, and re-sequence regulated/larger-enterprise buyers rather than forcing launch.
Conversion is weak despite usageDetermine whether measurement, value, procurement, or price is the blocker before hiring sales.
A better capital path emergesUse pilot revenue, grants/non-dilutive capital, strategic partners, or a smaller raise if the full $650K is unnecessary.
07 · Bottom Line

The Ask in One Sentence

Investment askAgent First is currently planning a $650,000 pre-seed round to fund paid customer proof, recurring conversion, repeatable implementation, and production/security readiness — with financing terms left open until investor and counsel discussions.

The company should not spend to the memo merely because capital is available. If pilots, non-dilutive funding, strategic partners, or faster conversion reduce the amount required to reach the proof milestones, the company should preserve that discipline.
