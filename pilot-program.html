Security Readiness Gate

Before production data enters Agent First, the environment must earn it.

Agent First can be demonstrated, configured, benchmarked, and tested with fictional or appropriately de-identified material. Before proprietary production procedures, client-specific operating material, sensitive production context, or regulated information enters a live pilot, Agent First completes a focused security architecture and application-security review with an experienced security-minded SaaS engineer or appsec consultant.

  • Threat model and architecture/data-flow map.
  • Server-side Agent, Supervisor, and Admin authorization, plus company/tenant and Client Profile isolation testing.
  • Review of authentication, sessions, APIs and database, secrets, uploads, AI/model-provider data flow, logs, backups, retention and deletion, dependencies, and incident response.
  • OWASP ASVS 5.0 mapping, critical and high finding remediation, retest, and a signed data boundary.
Not SOC 2 tomorrow: the sequence is prototype and demo, then security architecture review, then critical remediation, then a hardened pilot environment, then real production pilots, then formal compliance when buyers actually require it.
What happens

Nine steps from interest to operating decision.

Step 1
Scope the observation
Choose the bounded workflow, sponsor, representative users, permissions, and the question the pilot needs to answer.
Step 2
Observe real work
Watch a representative slice of the workflow before configuration. With approval, workflow-capture tooling can document systems, searches, handoffs, delays, workarounds, and decision points.
Step 3
Map decisions and sources
Connect each material decision to required context, exceptions, authority, approved sources, next actions, and escalation. Resolve differences between documented, observed, and required work.
Step 4
Lock the baseline
Measure the pre-intervention workflow using an agreed method before Agent First results are visible.
Step 5
Controlled configuration
Build Agent First around the observed decision moments using approved, fictional, or de-identified material where appropriate.
Step 6
Validation and Security Gate
Rule owners validate the guidance; required security and data gates pass before proprietary production use.
Step 7
Live pilot
Bounded use, weekly review, issue tracking, rule governance, and measurement.
Step 8
Final readout
Compare the baseline with results, limitations, issues, and what the workflow taught us.
Step 9
Decision
Continue, expand, revise and retest, or stop.
What gets measured

Five numbers, tracked before and after.

The baseline locked in step 4 and the readout in step 8 are built from the same five measures every Agent First pilot reports on, not a different scorecard per customer.

Decision resolution time
How long it takes to go from a question arriving to the correct, client-specific answer being given. Timestamped at the decision moment, before and after configuration.
Correct-client rule application
Whether the rule actually applied matches the rule that governs that specific client, account, or location. Sampled and checked against the source policy, not self-reported.
First-contact accuracy
Whether the first answer given was the right one, versus one that had to be corrected, escalated, or redone on a follow-up contact.
Supervisor pull-ins
How often a frontline worker needed a supervisor or senior teammate to resolve something a correct, available answer could have handled directly.
New-hire ramp time
How long it takes a new or reassigned worker to reach full independent accuracy on the piloted workflow, compared to the pre-pilot baseline.
How it's collected
A rotating sample of sessions each week, not continuous monitoring of every worker: live shadowing, recorded-interaction review, supervisor pull-in logs, and Agent First's own decision and audit trail, cross-checked against each other.
