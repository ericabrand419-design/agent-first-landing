01

Executive Summary

Agent First marketing should create qualified customer conversations and turn real proof into repeatable demand. The first motion is founder-led, evidence-led, and intentionally narrow. The company should prefer a small number of relevant accounts, strong discovery, interactive product education, and measured pilots over broad paid acquisition.

Operating rule: current product messaging is evaluation-ready for controlled fictional/de-identified workflows. Production-pilot ready is used only after the Security Readiness Gate passes for the scoped proprietary-data environment.
02

Marketing Thesis

Do not sell “AI improves productivity.” Name the operating failure: frontline workers are asked to search, remember, compare, interpret, and translate rules while serving customers. Agent First reframes the problem around the decision a worker must make in a specific client context.

Problem first

Knowledge friction, wrong-context risk, supervisor dependence.

Decision first

Correct context, approved rule, wording, source and next action.

Evidence first

Baselines, Test Mode, pilots and measured readouts outrank generic claims.

03

Audience / ICP

Primary wedge: multi-client contact centers and outsourced/shared-service teams with repeated context switching, approved source material, a rule owner, representative users, measurable baseline and a workflow that can be proven without major integration.

04

Positioning & Messaging

One-sentence positioning: Agent First is operational decision support for rules-heavy frontline teams: it puts the correct client context, approved rule, wording, source, and next step behind the worker while keeping the human in control.

Tagline: Keep the human. Upgrade the agent.   AI framing: AI behind the agent.   Security philosophy: Right information. Right client. Right moment. Right person.

06

Buyer Journey & Funnel

Awareness → Education → Controlled Evaluation → Business Case → Security / Risk Review → Paid Production Pilot → Final Readout → Subscription / Expansion or Stop.

Awareness

Founder POV, Agent Challenge, problem pages.

Evaluation

Deal Room, product video, private demo, Test Mode, ROI tool.

Proof

Pilot scorecard, weekly review, final report, conversion proposal.

07

Customer Deal Room

The default answer to “send me something” is one private link. Recommended customer path: Start Here → Product Video → Private Demo → Pilot Prospectus → Business Case → Implementation → Security & Data → Pricing → FAQ.

08

Thought Leadership & Content Engine

Core pillars: agent-first design vs automation-first design; knowledge storage vs moment-of-action usability; context switching; training/ramp; rule authority; responsible AI/security; pilot measurement; product learnings; customer proof when earned.

09

Targeted Outbound

Use small researched account lists. Lead with a concrete workflow observation, invite workflow discovery rather than a generic AI demo, and disqualify when there is no sponsor, source authority, measurable baseline, bounded scope, or credible security path.

10

Lead Nurture & Lifecycle

Demo request → owner review → approved activation → controlled-evaluation guidance → discovery recap → pilot/security materials when relevant → measured pilot → conversion or stop. Avoid generic “checking in” sequences.

Security sales enablement: the Security / Data / Governance Package is now the canonical buyer-diligence artifact for current-state data flow, data inventory, preliminary vendors, rule governance and questionnaire responses. It must be presented as current-state documentation—not independent assurance.
11

Sales Enablement

Canonical assets: customer one-pager, product overview/video, Pilot Prospectus, pricing, implementation, Security/Data + Security Readiness + Security/Data/Governance Package / questionnaire library, FAQ, ROI tool, industry use cases, Sample Client Profile, and later final reports/case studies. The Deal Room is the versioning layer.

12

Pilot Marketing & Conversion

The pilot is a commercial proof product. Set baseline and success measures before results, preserve security and scope gates during implementation, then make the final readout honest enough to support continue, expand, revise, or stop.

13

Customer Proof

No fabricated case studies. Publish only permissioned evidence that states baseline, intervention, sample/workflow, duration, result, limitations and implementation context.

14

Video & Demo Strategy

Use a 30–45 second problem hook, 60–90 second product overview, 5–7 minute guided customer demo, Test Mode demonstration, founder video, and a security segment for serious buyers. Replace mock footage with real product capture as the private product stabilizes.

15

Partners / Advisors / Ecosystem

Test contact-center consultants, BPO advisors, training/QA firms, security/application-security experts, accelerators and technical partners based on qualified buyer access, implementation leverage, credibility or technical acceleration.

16

Measurement & Attribution

Track qualified discovery, discovery→demo, demo→pilot proposal, proposal→paid pilot, days to decision, pilot completion, pilot→subscription, implementation time/cost, referenceability, expansion and source. Vanity traffic and follower counts are secondary.

17

Marketing Operations / CRM

Minimum fields: account/contact/opportunity, lifecycle stage, source, buyer role, workflow pain, incumbent stack, security/procurement requirements, next action/date, Deal Room engagement, pilot economics and reference permission.

18

Budget & Resourcing

High founder time / low cash for thought leadership; maintain website/Deal Room as productized sales infrastructure; small targeted outbound spend; selective travel/events; defer broad paid media and dedicated marketing hire until a channel and ICP begin repeating.

12-Month Roadmap

Q1: synchronized story + pipeline + security messaging. Q2: first paid production pilot + measured objections/evidence. Q3: multiple paid pilots / first recurring conversion target + case study if earned. Q4: repeatable acquisition path + stronger proof library + decision on dedicated GTM capacity.

21

Risks & Guardrails

Guard against AI hype outrunning product, security claims outrunning controls, equal focus across too many industries, generic content, vanity-metric spending, collateral drift, and case-study pressure. The Deal Room remains canonical; every major claim must be classified as current fact, working assumption, target, or future plan.
