Investor FAQ · v1.0

Direct answers to hard questions.

The complete downloadable FAQ contains 20 diligence questions; this web version highlights the core investor set.

01

What exactly is Agent First?

Operational decision support for rules-heavy frontline work. It surfaces the decision, wording, next step, source, and internal guidance for the active context.

02

Is this just another agent-assist product?

It overlaps with established categories. The wedge to prove is profile-first, decision-first guidance for client-specific operational rules, with governance and testing built into the operating model.

03

Who buys first?

Multi-client contact centers and outsourced/shared-service teams with repeated context switching, approved source material, a rule owner, representative users, and a measurable baseline.

04

Why not Five9 / Genesys / NiCE / Cresta / Observe.AI / Salesforce / ServiceNow / Glean?

Many offer overlapping capabilities. Agent First is not claiming they cannot. The differentiation hypothesis is the Client Profile/rule model, explicit context switching, governance, built-in measurement, and lightweight configuration-first entry.

05

What is the pilot model?

90-day live pilot at $69 per pilot agent/month; $2,500 Client Profile implementation fee waived; no Growth discount during pilot; $500 first-invoice conversion credit.

06

What is actually built?

Working private demo, five industries / ten fictional organizations, Agent/Supervisor/Admin roles, Client Profiles, scripts/sources/memory, Test Mode, Guided Demo, private access, regression tests, pilot package, Business Plan, Financial Model, Deal Room, and investor materials.

07

What is not proved?

Customer ROI, paid-pilot close rate, conversion, retention, repeatable production implementation economics, repeatable sales cycle, expansion, external case studies, and enterprise certifications.

08

How big is the market?

BLS provides a 2.666M-job U.S. CSR labor-scale proxy. Agent First’s true serviceable market still requires bottom-up account research.

09

Why $650K?

Base financial model shows about $502K peak cumulative cash need. $650K adds roughly $148K of timing/uncertainty cushion and is intended to buy paid proof, recurring conversion, repeatable implementation, and production/security readiness.

10

What are the financing terms?

Only the planning target and use-of-funds logic are set. Instrument, valuation/cap, minimum check, investor rights, and governance remain TBD with counsel and prospective investors.

11

What is the next proof point?

A carefully selected paid pilot with a real rule owner, baseline, representative users, bounded workflow, and a continuation decision.
