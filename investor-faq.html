Investor FAQ · v1.2

Direct answers to hard questions.

01

What exactly is Agent First?

Operational decision support for rules-heavy frontline work. It surfaces the decision, wording, next step, source, and internal guidance for the active context.

02

Is this just another agent-assist product?

It overlaps with established categories. The wedge to prove is profile-first, decision-first guidance for client-specific operational rules, with governance and testing built into the operating model.

03

Who buys first?

Multi-client contact centers and outsourced/shared-service teams with repeated context switching, approved source material, a rule owner, representative users, and a measurable baseline.

04

What is the pilot model?

Controlled evaluation can begin with fictional/de-identified material. A production-data live pilot runs up to 90 days after operational and Security Readiness sign-off at $69 per pilot agent/month, with a 10-seat minimum ($690/month), the $2,500 Client Profile fee waived, no Growth discount, and a $500 credit on the first post-pilot invoice.

05

What security work happens before a production pilot?

Agent First will engage an experienced security-minded SaaS engineer or appsec consultant to produce a threat model and architecture/data-flow map; test server-side role and tenant/Client Profile isolation; review auth, sessions, APIs/database, secrets, uploads, AI/model-provider data flow, logs, backups, retention/deletion, dependencies and incident response; map against OWASP ASVS 5.0; fix critical findings; retest; and document the pilot data boundary.

06

What is actually built?

Working private demo, five industries / ten fictional organizations, Agent/Supervisor/Admin roles, Client Profiles, scripts/sources/memory, Test Mode, Guided Demo, private access, regression tests, pilot package, Business Plan, Financial Model, Deal Room, investor package and Security Readiness Plan.

07

What is not proved?

Customer ROI, paid-pilot close rate, conversion, retention, repeatable production implementation economics, repeatable sales cycle, expansion, external case studies, Security Readiness Gate completion, and enterprise certifications.

08

Why $650K?

The revised Base financial model shows about $577K peak cumulative cash need after explicit Security Readiness Gate, remediation/retest and documentation costs. $650K leaves about $73K / 13% cushion and is intended to buy security readiness, paid production proof, recurring conversion, repeatable implementation and evidence.

09

What are the financing terms?

Only the planning target and use-of-funds logic are set. Instrument, valuation/cap, minimum check, investor rights, and governance remain TBD with counsel and prospective investors.

10

What is the next proof point?

Complete the Security Readiness Gate while controlled evaluation continues with fictional/de-identified material, then run a carefully selected paid production pilot with a real rule owner, baseline, representative users, bounded workflow and continuation decision.
