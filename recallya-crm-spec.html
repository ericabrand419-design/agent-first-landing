Recallya Internal CRM Operating Specification

How Agent First should dogfood Recallya as its CRM and relationship operating system without allowing a young internal product to become a single point of operational failure.

Internal dogfooding rule: Recallya should become Agent First’s first serious CRM operating environment, but Agent First must always retain exports, backups, and a manual fallback.

Core objects

Company

Legal/display name, segment, industry, size, geography, current relationship state, owner, source.

Contact

Person, role, buying influence, email/phone, consent/suppression state, relationship notes.

Opportunity / Pilot

Stage, problem, sponsor, workflow, seats, expected value, decision date, blockers, next action, contract state.

Activity

Email, meeting, call, note, document, promise, objection, next step, approval/audit metadata.

Customer / Renewal

Contract, billing contact, seats, conversion/renewal date, health, expansion signals, support history.

Evidence / Permission

Pilot outcome, source, limitations, customer permission, claim status, published destinations.

Pipeline

Lead → Qualified → Discovery → Private Demo → Pilot Application → Proposal → Contract → Onboarding → Pilot → Conversion → Customer → Renewal.

Ask Recallya

Record

What happened? What was promised? What did they buy? When did we last talk?

Interpret

What matters now? What objections are recurring? Why is this stalled? What changed?

Act

What should happen next? Draft the reply. Prepare the meeting. Create the task. Send only within the configured approval mode.

Controlled agency

HUMAN

System prepares context; human performs the action.

APPROVAL

System drafts/proposes; human explicitly approves before sending or committing.

AUTOPILOT

Only for bounded, pre-approved, reversible actions with an audit trail and kill switch.

Negotiated pricing, contract changes, security answers, unusual promises, material commitments, and sensitive customer decisions should remain Human/Approval by default.

Email sequence behavior

  • Reply stops the active sequence and creates a human-review state.
  • Booking a meeting stops the sales sequence and moves the workflow.
  • Clicks/engagement may change next action but should not invent customer intent.
  • Personalization comes from the relationship record, not unsupported inference.
  • Commercial email must maintain suppression/opt-out controls and accurate sender information.

Dashboards

Founder pipeline

Qualified opportunities, next actions, stalled records, decision dates, forecast category, blockers.

Pilot operations

Launch gate, baseline status, measurement cadence, issues, final readout, conversion state.

Customer / renewal

Health, usage, open commitments, billing, renewal dates, expansion/at-risk signals.

Mandatory resilience

  • Export companies, contacts, opportunities, activities, contracts, and key relationship summaries.
  • Do not trap executed agreements, invoices, or legal/security records only inside Recallya.
  • Maintain periodic backup/export and a basic spreadsheet/manual fallback for critical revenue operations.
  • Audit what Recallya sent, under which mode/rule, and who approved it when approval was required.
