Why tools instead of a download

A checklist tells you what to think about. A tool, or a game, shows you what's actually happening in your own operation, or what it feels like on the floor, in a couple of minutes. Agent First exists to solve an operational problem, so these are built the same way: small, working, and honest about what they can and can't tell you.
